# KB0010011: Microsoft Teams audio, calls and screen sharing

**Category:** Collaboration  
**Assignment group:** Messaging  
**Resolved example tickets:** INC0010090, INC0010106, INC0010124, INC0010146, INC0010148

## Symptoms
No audio in Teams meetings, calls drop after a few minutes, or screen sharing shows a black screen.

## Cause
Wrong audio device selected, an outdated Teams client, or the VPN routing Teams traffic through the tunnel.

## Resolution
1. In Teams, Settings > Devices, pick the right speaker and microphone and make a test call.
2. Update Teams (profile menu > Check for updates) and restart it.
3. Clear the Teams cache: quit Teams, delete the contents of %APPDATA%\Microsoft\Teams, and reopen.
4. If calls drop only on VPN, confirm split tunnelling for Teams is on (see KB0010001).
