# KB0010010: Printing and fax problems

**Category:** Hardware  
**Assignment group:** Hardware  

## Tickets

- INC0000008: Printer in my office is out of toner (Closed)
- INC0000006: Hangs when trying to print VISIO document (Closed)
- INC0000012: Customer didn't receive eFax (Closed)

## Symptoms
Printer out of toner, the app hangs when printing one kind of document, or a fax didn't arrive.

## Cause
Consumables run out, a stuck print queue or bad printer driver, or a failed fax job or wrong number.

## Resolution
1. Toner or paper: send a supply order or replace the cartridge, and check the printer's status page.
2. App hangs when printing: clear the print queue, restart the Print Spooler service, and update the printer driver.
3. Try printing the same file to PDF. If that works, the problem is the printer driver.
4. Fax not received: check the fax server's log for the job, confirm the number, and resend.

## Notes
Toner requests should go through the supplies catalog item, not an incident.
