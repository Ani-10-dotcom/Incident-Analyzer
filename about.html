How it works

The AI Incident Analyzer lists ServiceNow incidents by priority, flags duplicate and similar tickets, and charts ticket status by week. It runs on 67 incidents exported from the ServiceNow developer instance dev460097 on Oct 29, 2026.

Architecture

All figures come from one dataset, so every page agrees. The site is static: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with d3 v7.9.0, served by Vercel with no build step.

ServiceNow CSV export→data/incidents.js→Calculations (similarity, summary, weekly)→Dashboard and Known issues pages

Features

Summary tiles. All tickets, New, In Progress, Pending and Resolved counts, plus a one-line takeaway of tickets waiting on the team.

Tickets tab. Every incident in one table, sorted P1 to P5, newest first within each priority. Filters: From and To dates (on Opened), Show (one status group) and a free-text Search over number, description, group and reference ticket. Reset clears them all.

Duplicate and Similar matching. Each ticket is compared with every earlier ticket:

  1. The short description is lower-cased, punctuation removed, and common words (the, to, on, is…) dropped. Endings "-ing" and "-s" are trimmed so "emails" matches "email".
  2. Similarity is the share of words two tickets have in common (shared words divided by all distinct words in both).
  3. The earlier ticket with the highest score becomes the Reference ticket.
  4. Duplicate: 70% or more alike and opened within 3 days of the reference. Similar: 30% or more alike, at any distance. Below 30% the Match column is blank.

Clicking a ticket opens its details: every field, its match and reference ticket, later tickets that match it, the closest known-issue article with its suggested fix, and a link to open the record in ServiceNow. Clicking a reference ticket in the table scrolls to that ticket.

Status of tickets tab. A stacked bar chart of tickets opened each week, split by their status today (Resolved, Pending, In Progress, New).

  • Weeks are weeks of the month: week 1 is days 1 to 7, week 2 days 8 to 14, week 3 days 15 to 21, week 4 days 22 to 28, week 5 the rest of the month.
  • Year defaults to the latest year with tickets. Month narrows to one month. Week shows only that week, in every month shown.
  • Weeks with no tickets show as empty, so gaps are visible.
  • Hovering a bar shows its counts and date span.

Known issues. Sixteen resolution articles (KB0010001 to KB0010016) written from the tickets in the export, with symptoms, cause and resolution steps. Each article lists its tickets by number, and every ticket in the export belongs to exactly one article. A ticket that no article lists (for example from a newer export) gets a suggested article by word match, 30% or more alike.

ServiceNow integration

The data is a CSV snapshot. A live ServiceNow connection is not working yet, so the site is refreshed from a new export.

Refreshing the data

  1. Open https://dev460097.service-now.com/incident_list.do?CSV (or open incident_list.do and use Export > CSV from a column header menu).
  2. Convert it to the seven fields above, priority mapped to P1 to P5 and caller and assignee names dropped, and replace data/incidents.js and data/incidents.json.
  3. Push to main. Vercel redeploys on its own.
